Collect every gift and the door at the end opens. The arrows walk and climb, Alt or Z fires the jetpack, and Ctrl, Enter or X runs the Phase Shifter, which tunnels through brick but not grey stone. Hold Down while flying to keep your height, and press Down on a button or teleporter to use it. Esc loses this Santa when you're stuck, + and − change the game speed, P pauses and M turns the sound off. When the game ends, put your name on the high score board. Press H on the title screen to see it.
